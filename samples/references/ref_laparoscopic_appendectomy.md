# Reference summary: Going home after keyhole surgery to remove your appendix

*Clinician-approved teaching example — synthetic. Use for tone and structure, not as patient facts.*

## What happened in hospital
You came to hospital with pain in the lower right side of your tummy. Tests showed that your appendix
was swollen and infected. This is called appendicitis. The surgeons removed your appendix using
keyhole surgery. This means they made three small cuts instead of one large one. The operation went well.

## About your condition
The appendix is a small pouch attached to your bowel. You can live a normal, healthy life without it.
Once it has been removed, appendicitis cannot come back.

## Your medicines
Your care team has listed each medicine, how much to take and when, in the table on this summary.
Pain relief helps you move around, which speeds up healing. If you were given an antibiotic, take every
dose until the course is finished, even if you feel better. Stopping early can let the infection return.

## Looking after your wounds
Keep the small wounds clean and dry. You may shower after two days. Pat the wounds dry gently. Do not
soak in a bath or swim until the wounds have fully healed. Some bruising around the wounds is normal.

## Activity and eating
Eat normally, starting with light meals. Walking every day helps prevent chest infections and blood
clots. Avoid lifting anything heavy, such as a full bucket of water or shopping bags, for about two
weeks. Most people return to office work within one to two weeks.

## When to get help urgently
Go to the emergency department or call the hospital straight away if you have a fever, pain that gets
worse instead of better, redness, swelling or pus coming from a wound, vomiting that will not stop,
or if you cannot pass urine.

## Your follow-up
You will have a check-up with the surgical team to look at your wounds. Bring this summary with you.

## Questions you might want to ask
- When can I drive again?
- When can I go back to work or the gym?
- Who do I call if I am worried at night?
