# Reference summary: Going home with heart failure

*Clinician-approved teaching example — synthetic. Use for tone and structure, not as patient facts.*

## What happened in hospital
You came to hospital because fluid had built up in your body, making you breathless and causing your
legs to swell. This happens when the heart does not pump as strongly as it should. This is called
heart failure. Medicines helped your body get rid of the extra fluid.

## About your condition
Heart failure does not mean your heart has stopped. It means your heart needs support to work well.
With the right medicines and daily habits, many people live full lives with this condition.

## Daily weight check
Weigh yourself every morning after using the toilet and before breakfast. Write it down. A sudden
gain of more than two kilograms in three days can mean fluid is building up again.

## Your medicines
Your care team has listed each medicine, how much to take and when, in the table on this summary.
Some heart medicines make you pass more urine. Do not stop them without talking to your doctor.

## When to get help urgently
Get help straight away if you are breathless at rest or when lying flat, have chest pain, faint,
or your weight goes up quickly.
